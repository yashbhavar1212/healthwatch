    What happens to one report

    1. The health worker reports

      Village, diarrhea and fever cases, rainfall and the state of drinking water. It takes under a minute on a phone.

    2. The system scores it

      Points are added for each factor, plus one more if cases are rising compared with the last report. The total becomes Low, Moderate, High or Critical.

    3. Officers see it and act

      The village shows as High or Critical on the dashboard, an alert appears, and an e-mail can go out. Each alert says what to do next.
